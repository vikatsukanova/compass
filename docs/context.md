# Compass — Context

## Product vision

Compass helps you maintain balance across the parts of life that matter to you. Each morning, you open the app and see **Today's Focus** — not a overwhelming task list, but a clear picture of which life areas need attention today and why.

The app tracks how you actually spend time (manual logs) against weekly targets you set per area. It surfaces neglect, being behind pace, staying on track, and over-focusing when one area dominates while others suffer.

## User problem

Without a system, it's easy to:

- Spend a week deep in work and realize Health, Learning, or Social got zero attention
- Chase todos without noticing that high-priority life areas are starving
- Feel guilty about "productivity" instead of asking "am I living in balance?"

Spreadsheets and generic todo apps don't answer: *"Am I neglecting something important this week?"*

## Target user

People who want to track life areas and weekly goals, notice imbalance, and choose where to focus. Each deployment serves one account, with a calm morning ritual and intentional manual tracking.

## Life areas

Default areas for MVP (editable later):

| Area | Example weekly target |
|------|------------------------|
| Health | 3 sessions |
| Career | 40 hours |
| Learning | 5 hours |
| Creativity | 2 sessions |
| Home | 1 session |
| Social | 3 activities |

Each area has:

- **Weekly target** — numeric goal (hours, sessions, or count — unit TBD per area)
- **Priority** — high, medium, or low (drives Today's Focus ordering)

## What the app should feel like

- **Calm and clear** — morning-friendly, not noisy
- **Honest** — shows imbalance without shaming ("neglected" not "failed")
- **Action-oriented** — Today's Focus suggests what to do today, not just stats
- **Fast to log** — logging an activity should take seconds
- **Balance-first** — the dashboard highlights distribution, not just completion rate

## What is intentionally out of scope (MVP)

- Fitbit or other health tracker integration
- Google Calendar or external calendar sync
- AI-generated plans or smart scheduling
- Push notifications or reminders
- Multi-user or cloud sync
- Generic project management (Kanban, Gantt, etc.)
- Gamification, streaks, or guilt-inducing "you missed 5 days" messaging
- Automatic time tracking
